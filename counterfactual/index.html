Counterfactual model

What the wait may have cost

An estimate, not a count of real events. If bedroom consent-and-safety technology had been built and adopted after the original provisional patent application was filed on January 3, 2015, how many date rapes and dating sexual assaults could it have prevented? The model counts only those, not every sexual assault: some kinds, like assault on someone who is asleep, a bedroom system couldn’t easily stop. The answer depends entirely on the assumptions below, so every one of them is shown and adjustable.

These clocks aren't a measure of blame. They show the pace: how much harm accrues while a fix waits, even over a few days, and why this needs warp-speed priority rather than the usual pace of institutions.

N is the preventable harm overall; s is the share attributable to the inventor, as opposed to the institutions, industry and funders that declined to build or adopt the technology.

How it's calculated

N(t) = ∫STARTt A × d × b × u(τ) × e dτ

Adoption u(τ) rises in a straight line from 0 at the start date to umax at the year adoption peaks, then stays flat. The integral is computed in closed form, so the estimate rises smoothly in real time. With D the length of the ramp in years and x the years since the start:

  • During the ramp: N = A × d × b × e × umax × x² ÷ (2D)
  • After the ramp: N = A × d × b × e × umax × (D ÷ 2 + years since the peak)
  • After the peak, the rate is A × d × b × umax × e per year.

Limitations

  • Adoption and efficacy are hypothetical. No bedroom consent-and-safety system has been deployed at scale or tested in a randomized trial; Mission Zero's proposed pilot is meant to measure this.
  • The model counts only date rapes and dating sexual assaults. It leaves out assaults by strangers and family members, and assaults that begin while the person is asleep or already unconscious, which a system that depends on a word, a button or a renewed yes couldn’t easily interrupt. Other proposals on this site address some of them.
  • Not every date or dating assault is reachable by a bedroom system. Assaults in cars, outdoors, at work or online fall outside it.
  • Survey estimates of sexual assault vary widely by method, wording and population, so the annual figure A is uncertain.
  • The model assumes a constant annual rate and a simple linear adoption ramp; real adoption and prevention would be uneven.
  • The averted-cost figure multiplies the estimate by a per-victim lifetime cost for rape, in 2014 dollars, and is not adjusted for inflation or for differences between types of assault. Because the survey counts victimizations rather than victims, and includes threatened and attempted assaults, it may overstate the cost.
